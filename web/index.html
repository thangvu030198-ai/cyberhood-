05 · FAQ

Before you connect

Is connecting my wallet safe?

We only ask for a signature on a plain-text message. It is free: no transaction, no gas, no token approval and no spending permission. We will never ask for your seed phrase or private key — anyone who does is a scam.

Which wallets work?

Any EVM wallet that runs in your browser: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet, Trust Wallet, Rainbow, Zerion, Phantom, Brave Wallet, Bitget Wallet and more. On a phone, open this page inside your wallet's browser, or paste your address by hand.

What does the Hunter Pass give me?

An account ID tied to your wallet. Early-access rewards, referral points and the Founding Hunter badge are credited to it when Season 0 launches.

Do I have to post on X?

Yes, the post is how early Hunters spread the word. We draft it for you; you can edit every word before it goes out.

Is the demo free?

Yes. The demo runs in the browser on desktop and phone. Your progress is saved to your profile.
